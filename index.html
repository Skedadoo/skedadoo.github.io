Who we are

Founded in 2011, Nick Enterprise is dedicated to supplying beautiful, timeless tile, marble, stone, granite, and mosaic. From grand homes to malls and commercial buildings, we work to meet every requirement, and to see every customer through to complete satisfaction.

We source directly from local suppliers and our partner factory in China, hand-selecting each tile and slab for durability, aesthetics, and sustainability. From material selection to custom cutting, tabletop installation, and design guidance, our team supports architects, interior designers, contractors, and homeowners from first idea to finished space.
